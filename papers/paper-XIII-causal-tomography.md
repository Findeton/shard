# Causal tomography: certifying manifoldlikeness of a finite causal order from two-dimensional shadows

**Author:** Felix Robles Elvira (ORCID: 0009-0009-2017-4394; independent researcher)

**Status:** preprint, not peer reviewed, version 2026-07-02. This paper is self-contained. The theorems (Sections 2–4) are elementary and proved in-paper given the named imports (Gallai's transitive-orientation theorem, Logan's finite-angle uncertainty principle); the numerical demonstrations are float64 measurement landscapes with fixed seed `default_rng(20260702)` and measured-and-disclosed tolerances, and the repository ships the complete demonstration code and canonical outputs (receipts `i1`–`i3`, `k1`–`k3`, `l1`–`l3`, `m1` in `code/`, outputs in `receipts/`). Claims are graded in place: **[THEOREM]** = proved here; **[DEMONSTRATED]** = measured with shipped code at the stated scale; verdicts that fall short of a gate are labeled **inconclusive**, not certified.

## Abstract

Whether a finite partial order is "manifoldlike" — approximable by a uniform-density sprinkling of a Lorentzian spacetime — is the causal-set form of the Hauptvermutung, and above `1+1` dimensions it collides with an order-theoretic wall: Minkowski dimension detaches from Dushnik–Miller order dimension, and recognition of the relevant order classes is believed hard. We do not attack the decision problem. We split **verification from search**: we construct a polynomial-time-verifiable *certificate* of higher-dimensional volume-faithfulness — possessing one is proof, up to an exactly characterized budget — and treat finding one as heuristic search whose hardness is expected and priced. Four results. **(1) A 2D engine [THEOREM].** For orders of dimension ≤ 2 we prove a per-order, deterministic, quantitative characterization: `P` admits a volume-faithful embedding into the `M²` diamond with anchored-box defect ≤ ε **iff** `dim(P) ≤ 2` and the *canonical rank embedding* — built order-intrinsically from the essentially unique realizer (Gallai) — has star discrepancy `D* ≤ 3ε + 1/n` (backward direction exact). A relabeling lemma shows the residual realizer freedom (modules) moves the embedded point set not at all; the one exception class (internal prime flips) is closed by census and priced below `10⁻⁵` where it occurs. **(2) Causal tomography [THEOREM + DEMONSTRATED].** The order of `M^{1+d−1}` is the intersection of its null-coordinate total orders over the celestial sphere; each antipodal direction pair projects the order to a 2D **shadow** whose target density is computable in closed form (the chord law `√((1−|t|)²−s²)` in `2+1`; the disk-section law `(1−|t|)²−s²` in `3+1`). Scoring shadows with a weighted discrepancy against the target copula, plus a **harmonic condition** — direction profiles of a faithful order are degree-≤1 (Fourier / spherical) harmonics whose coefficients *are* the coordinates — yields certificate conditions that reconstruct coordinates at correlation `0.9994` per axis in `2+1` and `0.9999` per axis in `3+1` (oracle-family), including, to our knowledge, the first reconstruction of the **celestial sphere of a causal set from inversion counts alone** (`S¹`: radius CV `0.009`; `S²`: radius CV `0.008`, angle correlation `0.9995`). **(3) The ghost budget [THEOREM-shaped + DEMONSTRATED].** The certificate's soundness is scoped exactly: every marginal-preserving rearrangement is either negligible or a spectral density modulation; a mode invisible to `K` directions must have frequency `q ≳ 1/sin(π/2K)` (Logan), and any such mode moves every anchored-box count by at most `∼a/q` — so the certificate controls the discrepancy metric at all scales, while high-frequency spectral non-uniformity below that amplitude is provably outside *any* finite-`K` discrepancy certificate. Both halves are demonstrated: a constructed `q = 120` ghost (genuinely non-uniform, matched-filter `z = 15.3`) passes the full certificate; the same amplitude at `q = 6` is caught at over twice the calibrated floor. **(4) An intrinsic finder [DEMONSTRATED].** A fully order-intrinsic search pipeline — a past/future-count temporal seed, a chain-fleet transverse embedding, an order-only scale calibration, and a *tomographic bootstrap* that uses the reconstruction operator as its own polish — collapses the search gap from `6×` the certificate floor (annealing from cold) to `1.5×` at `N = 400` and to `1.21×` at `N = 1000` in `2+1` — the latter *inside* the certificate gate for the shadow condition — and runs end-to-end in `3+1` at `1.50×` (verdict: inconclusive, at exact parity with the `2+1` pilot's first stage). The same pipeline *refuses* two non-manifoldlike adversary classes. Verification is solved at demonstration scale with its soundness budget stated exactly; search is measurably close; certified end-to-end `3+1` reconstruction is the stated open problem.

## 1. The problem and the reframe

A causal set is a locally finite partial order proposed as the deep structure of spacetime [1]. Its central mathematical problem is manifoldlikeness: which orders arise (with high probability, or approximately) as Poisson sprinklings of a Lorentzian manifold, with the order given by the causal relation [2, 3]? By Malament's theorem the causal order of a distinguishing spacetime determines its conformal geometry [4], and sprinkling density supplies the volume factor — "order + number = geometry". The verification form of the question, per order: *given one finite order `P`, decide — and quantify — whether it embeds in a spacetime region at uniform density.*

In `1+1` dimensions the order theory cooperates: embeddability in `M²` is exactly Dushnik–Miller dimension ≤ 2, recognizable in polynomial time [5, 6]. Above that the wall is real: the realizer route collapses (spacelike configurations of `M^{1+2}` induce circle containment orders, for whose recognition no polynomial algorithm is known and which are believed hard [7]), and the field's practical instruments — dimension estimators [8, 9], action-based tests [10], embedding procedures [11] — are either necessary conditions only, or procedures without theorems.

We therefore stop attacking symmetric decision and split the problem NP-style. A **certificate** of `d`-dimensional volume-faithfulness is a witness structure `W` such that *(T1)* verifying `W` is polynomial-time and yields a constructive embedding with a defect bound, and *(T2)* every faithful order possesses a `W` (its true geometric structure). Finding `W` is then honest heuristic territory: worst-case hard, as it must be — but *any* success is a proof, not a score. Sections 2–3 build the certificate; Section 4 scopes its soundness exactly; Section 5 attacks the search face; Section 6 runs the `3+1` stack. Throughout, `D*` denotes anchored-box (star) discrepancy of a planar point set, and all gates, floors, and tolerances are calibrated and disclosed in the shipped receipts.

## 2. The two-dimensional engine

Everything downstream scores 2D projections, so the 2D case must be solved *per order*, deterministically and quantitatively. Prior results in this regime are typicality statements — the uniform measure on 2D orders concentrates on continuum-like orders [12], and random 2D orders are sprinklings of the diamond in distribution [13] — or embedding procedures without canonicity. We prove:

**Theorem 2.1 (canonical realizer).** A 2D order whose incomparability graph is prime (in the modular-decomposition sense) has exactly two transitive orientations (Gallai [14]), hence exactly one realizer pair `(L₁, L₂)` up to the parity swap `u ↔ v`. For non-prime orders the residual freedom is exactly the module freedom, and the **relabeling lemma** disposes of almost all of it: series modules admit no freedom, and a parallel block's quotient enters `L₁` in one order and `L₂` reversed, so reorienting it permutes which element carries which coordinate pair — the embedded point *set*, hence `D*`, is exactly invariant. Only *internal prime-node* flips can move the point set. *(Demonstrated: bit-level invariance on a population carrying `105` modules at `N = 396`; the internal-prime residue closed by census — zero point-moving modules on sprinkling and lattice populations; on the one population where the freedom is real, `143` point-moving blocks are exhibited and priced at worst `|ΔD*| < 10⁻⁵`. Receipts `i1`, `i3`.)*

**Theorem 2.2 (volume gauge).** A 2D order's embedding freedom is exactly a pair of monotone reparametrizations of its two null coordinates — the discrete conformal freedom. Demanding uniform marginals fixes both: the **canonical rank embedding** (each null coordinate replaced by its normalized rank) is the volume-normalized representative of the order's conformal class, realizing the order exactly with exactly uniform marginals.

**Theorem 2.3 (characterization).** For a 2D order `P` on `n` elements, both directions quantitative: *(forward)* if `P` admits a volume-faithful embedding with anchored-box defect `≤ ε`, then `dim(P) ≤ 2` and the canonical rank embedding has star discrepancy `D* ≤ 3ε + 1/n`; *(backward, exact)* if the canonical rank embedding has `D* = δ`, then `P` is `δ`-volume-faithful — the rank embedding itself is such an embedding, and its defect *is* its `D*`. Forward proof: any ε-faithful embedding's coordinate orders are the canonical realizer up to parity and relabeling-invariant module freedom, and monotone reparametrization to uniform marginals moves anchored-box mass by ≤ 2ε, plus the `1/n` rank discretization.

The theorem is constructive and the pipeline is polynomial (dimension-2 recognition and modular decomposition are classical polynomial problems [6, 15]; exact `D*` is `O(n² log n)` here). The showcase measurement: monotone distortions `(u², √v)` of a faithful embedding leave the order bit-identical while inflating the raw point-set discrepancy `0.053 → 0.301`; the canonical rank embedding recovers the volume gauge *from the order alone* at `D* = 0.036` (receipt `i2`). Two honest boundaries, both measured: a bounded blow-up adversary (clusters of `c` co-located points) is flagged at finite `N` as a `√c` `D*`-inflation *diagnostic* (measured `2.45×` against the predicted `√6 = 2.45`) but is asymptotically volume-faithful — its outright rejection belongs to a separate *statistical* layer (interval-count dispersion; measured kill at `z = 28`); and volume-faithful ≠ Poisson-typical — a jittered lattice rightly passes the geometric characterization and fails the statistical layer (`z = 5.2` sub-ensemble dispersion). The characterization is a theorem about geometry; Poisson typicality is a model question deliberately split off.

## 3. Causal tomography in 2+1

**Theorem 3.1 (shadows).** For `P` the order of a finite point set in `M^{1+(d−1)}`: for every spatial unit vector `m`, the projection `x ↦ (t, m·x⃗)` maps `P` to a 2D dominance order that coarsens `P`, with null coordinates the functionals `u_{±m} = t ∓ m·x⃗`; and `P` is exactly the intersection of its shadow orders over the continuum celestial sphere (per pair, the supremum is achieved at `m = Δx⃗/|Δx⃗|`). *(The coarsening half is exact in-receipt; the intersection identity's finite-`K` face is measured — a `K`-direction grid intersection converges to `P` from above, extra relations `32/11/1` of `17,291` comparable pairs at `K = 16/32/64`. Receipt `k1`.)*

A faithful diamond sprinkling's shadow is not uniform: its density is the **chord law** `ρ(t, s) ∝ √((1−|t|)² − s²)` (verified at total variation `0.023` against a 200k-point projection). The Section 2 certifier generalizes in one move: score each shadow's point set against the *target copula* (the chord law pushed through its own quantile transforms — which also absorbs any monotone gauge freedom per direction) with a weighted star discrepancy `wD*`, floor-calibrated by same-law subsamples. Measured at `N = 400`: true shadows sit at `1.40×` the two-sample floor (gate `1.5×`), random-extension garbage at `9.4×`; the fine print is printed — the chord copula's departure from uniformity is small (`0.040` asymptotically), so at this `N` the weighting earns its keep in defect attribution and at scale, not as a dramatic finite-`N` kill.

**The harmonic condition.** For true Minkowski coordinates, `u_θ(x) = t − x cos θ − y sin θ`: the direction profile of every point is *exactly* a degree-≤1 Fourier polynomial in `θ`, and the coefficients `(a₀, −a₁, −b₁)` are `(t, x, y)`. The certificate condition demands quantile-gauged profiles (each `u_θ` recovered intrinsically from ranks through the target marginal) be degree-≤1 concentrated. Measured: RMS residual `0.020`; coordinate reconstruction at correlation `0.9994` per axis; the rebuilt point set reproduces the original order on `99.67%` of pairs and matches anchored-box counts to a mean absolute defect of `2.1` elements. Faithfulness transfers to the reconstruction with no closed-form volume formula anywhere.

**The celestial circle from inversion counts.** Classical multidimensional scaling on pairwise Kendall (inversion) distances between the `16` null-direction extensions recovers `S¹` — radius CV `0.009`, cyclic adjacency `16/16` — from order data alone, *given the extension family*. Finding the family intrinsically is the search face (Section 5).

**Two measured honesty points.** The certifier has a *plateau*: 400 adjacent-swap corruptions of the extensions leave the score at the floor, and corrupting all 16 directions still reconstructs coordinates at `> 0.999` per axis — certificate-grade families are interchangeable for reconstruction, which is aligned with the certificate's semantics (any certified witness yields the geometry). And annealing from a random start stalls at `6.0×` the floor after 12k local moves — the hardness face, exactly where the reframe put it. *(Receipts `k2`, `k3` — the latter also demonstrates the chain-fleet transverse principle of Section 5: greedy chain extraction, cross-chain comparability coupling, MDS recovers the transverse plane at Procrustes `0.993`, shuffled-coupling control `0.19`.)*

## 4. Certificate v2 and the ghost budget

**Certificate v2.** For a `d`-dimensional claim over direction set `{m_k}`: **(i)** every antipodal shadow scores within a max-calibrated same-law floor (gate `1.3×` the floor in `2+1`, `1.35×` in `3+1`, replicate-calibrated); **(ii)** harmonic residual `< 0.05`; **(iii)** the harmonic reconstruction *realizes the input order* at pair agreement `≥ 0.98`. Verdict bands, fixed in advance: `< 1.3×` certified; `1.3–2×` inconclusive; `> 2×` refused. Condition (iii) is what makes soundness discussable — the certificate asserts *this order embeds as this point set*, not merely *some projections look right* — and it is measurably load-bearing: a **separator adversary** (an extension family lifted from a *different* faithful configuration) passes (i) at `0.040` and (ii) at `0.012` and fails (iii) at agreement `0.794` (receipt `l1`).

**The soundness budget, closed into a loop.** Discrete tomography's classical ghosts — switching components [16, 17] — dichotomize here: small-support rearrangements leave the configuration faithful up to negligible defect (the certificate rightly still passes — the input is simply another faithful order), while extended switching patterns *are* density modulations. So the true adversary is spectral: `1 + a·cos(q⃗·x⃗)`. Two elementary facts pin its fate. *(a)* Its shadow marginal in direction `n_k` is fiber-averaged, leaving relative leakage `∼ a/(q·sin∠(q̂, n_k)·ℓ)` — so invisibility to all `K` directions forces `q ≳ 1/sin(π/2K)`: Logan's finite-angle uncertainty principle [18] in our setting. *(b)* Any mode's contribution to any anchored-box count — in a shadow or in the bulk — is at most `∼a/q`, because the CDF of an oscillation is flat to `1/q`. Together: **certificate-invisible ghosts cannot move the discrepancy metric anywhere**; what they can move is spectral density below wavelength `2π/q`. Demonstrated on all four legs (receipt `l1`): a `q = 120` ghost at `a = 0.6` is genuinely present (matched filter `z = 15.3`); it passes the full v2 certificate at `K = 8` *and* its shadows at `K = 16` (more directions do not catch it, exactly as *(b)* predicts) while bulk box discrepancy stays within its disclosed gate (`0.027 → 0.039`); and the same amplitude at `q = 6` is caught at over `2×` the floor. **The guarantee, final form:** certificate v2 implies an order-realizing embedding with anchored-box volume defect controlled at all scales; spectral non-uniformity above frequency `∼K` *can be* invisible whenever its amplitude sits below the CDF floor — a budget that is metric-intrinsic (it binds any discrepancy-type certificate), amplitude-conditioned, and extendable by an explicit spectral axis (matched filters per frequency band) for callers who need it.

## 5. The intrinsic finder

The search face closes to near-certificate range with a fully order-intrinsic pipeline, no annealing: **(1)** temporal seed `t̂ = |past(x)|^{1/d} − |future(x)|^{1/d}` with `d` the spacetime dimension (cone-volume counting: `1/3` in `2+1`, `1/4` in `3+1`); **(2)** transverse seed from a greedy chain fleet — extract long chains, form the cross-chain comparability coupling `w(c, c′)`, embed chains by MDS, extend to all elements by coupling-weighted averaging; **(3)** an order-only scale calibration (the MDS scale is arbitrary against `t̂`; a 12-point geometric scan on the certificate score fixes it — omitting this step scores `2.5×`, receipted); **(4)** the **tomographic bootstrap**: quantile-gauge each direction's profile through the target marginal, harmonically fuse into refined coordinates, iterate two to three times — the reconstruction operator as its own polish.

Measured in `2+1` (receipts `l2`, `m1`): at `N = 400`, `max wD* = 0.048` vs floor `0.033` — `1.5×`, inconclusive band, with condition (iii) at `0.953`; at `N = 1000` (`K = 12`), `0.0228` vs `0.0188` — **`1.21×`, inside the `1.3×` gate for condition (i)** — with (iii) at `0.9648`, still short of its `0.98` gate, so the overall verdict remains inconclusive and the residual is (iii)-only at this scale. The `1.5× → 1.21×` move measures the condition-(i) gap as finite-size, not structural. The refusal twin holds: the same pipeline refuses a Kleitman–Rothschild-type layered order (an explicit degenerate-fleet refusal path — such orders have no long-chain structure to seed from) and scores the cluster adversary at `2.2×` (refused band).

## 6. Three-plus-one dimensions

The stack transfers with no structural change (receipt `l3`; `N = 900`, `12` Fibonacci-spiral directions plus antipodes). The shadow target becomes the **disk-section law** `(1−|t|)² − s²` (TV `0.013`); all `12` true shadows pass at the max-calibrated floor (gate `1.35×`, 10-replicate calibration, pass margin `+0.10` floors — small, and said so; fairly quantile-mapped garbage scores `4.2×` above the true band); the harmonic condition becomes degree-≤1 *spherical* harmonics — profiles affine in `n ∈ S²`, residual `0.012` — whose four coefficients reconstruct `(t, x, y, z)` at `0.9999/0.9999/0.9998/0.9999` with the rebuilt order agreeing on `99.88%` of pairs. The flagship: MDS on pairwise inversion distances between the `24` signed-direction extensions yields a 3D shell of radius CV `0.008`, a decisively 3-dimensional spectrum (fourth-to-third eigenvalue ratio `0.019`, gated on `|λ₄|/λ₃`), and pairwise angles matching the true celestial angles at correlation `0.9995` — to our knowledge the first reconstruction of the celestial sphere of a causal set from inversion counts. These families are **oracle-derived** (built from the true coordinates, as a verification of the certificate, not a search result), and we say so.

The intrinsic pipeline of Section 5 then runs end-to-end in `3+1` on the same configuration (receipt `m1`): seed quality `t̂` correlation `0.961` and transverse Procrustes `0.888` from `M = 26` chains (the 4D chains are shorter and the transverse space larger — the seed bars are deliberately lower and disclosed); after calibration and bootstrap, `max wD* = 0.0372` vs floor `0.0247` — **`1.50×`, condition (iii) `0.970`: inconclusive, at exact parity with the `2+1` pilot's own first stage**. Held-out reconstruction: `t` correlation `0.974`; spatial correlation `0.916` after Procrustes alignment (the recovered spatial frame is defined only up to global rotation, so per-axis spatial correlations are undefined under the certificate's own symmetry). Given Section 5's scale lesson, the larger-`N` `3+1` run is a compute question, not a structural one — but the certified end-to-end `3+1` claim is **not made here**.

## 7. Limitations and open problems

All demonstrations are flat-diamond sprinklings at `N ≤ 1200`; curved backgrounds, boundaries, and non-diamond regions are untouched. The ghost budget's two legs are proved at the physics-paper level (fiber-averaging leakage; CDF flatness) with Logan cited for the principle; sharp constants and the finite-`N` noise-vs-leakage interplay are measured regimes, not theorems. The finder's refusal behavior is measured on two adversary classes, not proved. T1/T2 as stated in Section 1 are demonstrated constructively at pilot scale; their journal-grade quantitative forms (defect-propagation constants, the `S²` version of the harmonic argument) are open. The named open problems, in order of expected yield: (1) the certified end-to-end `3+1` reconstruction — close condition (iii)'s gap (`0.965/0.970` vs `0.98`) and run the `3+1` finder at larger `N`; (2) the sharp ghost-budget constants; (3) the statistical (Poisson-typicality) layer as a characterization rather than a battery of tests; (4) curved/conformally-flat extensions of the 2D engine.
